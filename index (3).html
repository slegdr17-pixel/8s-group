Operating principles

How the group intends to operate

I

Discipline first

II

Confirm, then commit

No order moves forward until terms, payment and delivery are agreed.

III

Honest by default

No invented clients, figures or stock. What is a concept is labelled a concept.

IV

Built to last

Classic over trendy. Slow, compounding growth across ventures.
